Table to kitchen to bill, in one app.

You're looking at Hosa Ruchi Kitchen & Café, a sample business in Bengaluru. A 13-table restaurant and café in Indiranagar with a second outlet in Jayanagar. Guests order from the table QR, the kitchen works station by station and the owner watches the day's sales live.

Plans & pricing How we built this app

How it works

  1. 1Guest scans the table QR and orders, or a captain takes the order
  2. 2The order becomes a KOT on the right kitchen station's screen
  3. 3The kitchen marks it ready; the captain serves
  4. 4Bill with GST and service charge; split or merge tables
  5. 5Pay by UPI, card or cash; the bill goes on WhatsApp
  6. 6Feedback and loyalty points; the owner sees the day's sales live

Try every role

Each login opens only that role's screens, just like the real product.

Guest
Priya Sharma (guest at table T4)

Scans the table QR, orders with veg / egg / non-veg marks, follows each dish live, asks for the bill, books a table or orders online.

Captain / waiter
Ravi Kumar (captain, indoor)

Works from the table map: takes orders with notes, serves what the kitchen marks ready, moves and merges tables, seats reservations and the waitlist.

Kitchen screen
Kitchen pass (4 stations)

A full-screen KOT board per station — tandoor, Chinese, main kitchen, bar — with timers, late colours, ready taps, printed KOTs and out-of-stock.

Cashier
Kavya Gowda (cashier)

Bills with 5% GST, discounts with a reason, split and merged bills, UPI / card / cash and part payments, the counter tokens, online and Swiggy / Zomato orders and the cash drawer.

Owner
Nikhil Shetty (owner)

The day's sales live, top dishes and hours, the bill edit log, menu and outlets, recipe stock, suppliers, wastage, guests, loyalty, feedback and staff shifts.

Delivery rider
Imran Pasha (delivery rider)

Gets the restaurant's own delivery orders, picks them up when the kitchen marks them ready and closes each one with the guest's OTP.

Swiggy / Zomato orders and e-invoices are simulated and labelled in this demo. Open the guest's QR menu in one tab and the kitchen screen in another to watch a KOT arrive.
