Reception
Rashmi Hegde (front desk)

Dentist
Dr. Kavya Nadig (dentist)

My chair today; examine on the 32-tooth chart with each tooth's history, build a treatment plan with cost per tooth and send it for approval, finish sittings, send crowns to the lab, take X-rays and photos, and write prescriptions from templates.
