01

Brief

A Bengaluru brokerage runs on four WhatsApp groups, a few portal logins and a notebook per agent. The same buyer calls from a 99acres ad on Monday and fills the website form on Thursday, and two agents chase them without knowing. Portal leads arrive by email and wait until someone copies them out; the agent who happens to be free gets the good ones. Site visits are booked on the phone and nobody can tell the owner whether a visit really happened or how it went. Offers go back and forth by text, the token is a UPI screenshot, and the brokerage — 1% from each side, a month's rent on a rental, a builder's commission on a new flat — sits in a spreadsheet until someone remembers to follow it up. Eleven months later the rent agreement ends and the tenant renews on their own.

DealOS runs the deal from enquiry to keys. An agent (or the owner, from their own app) adds a listing with photos, a video tour, a floor plan and amenities; it goes on the agency's website with buy / rent, BHK, budget and area filters, a map and a shareable brochure link, and is published to 99acres, MagicBricks and Housing (simulated in this demo). Enquiries from the website, portals, ads, walk-ins and channel partners land in one lead list; a second enquiry from the same phone is held as a duplicate and merged into the first, so a client who went quiet two months ago comes back as a returning client with their history. New leads go out by round robin with a score; the agent calls or WhatsApps in one tap, works the pipeline from new to booked and never misses a follow-up.

A site visit is booked in a slot and the buyer gets a 4-digit visit OTP. At the site the agent enters it and the phone's GPS: the owner sees the visit proved, 31 m from the property, and the buyer's feedback. Offers and counters move between the buyer's and the owner's apps; once the price is agreed the buyer pays the token and the deal opens with a payment schedule (resale, rental or construction-linked), the agreement documents, receipts and the brokerage due from each side. Channel partners register clients (protected for 60 days), see the builder project's tower and unit grid live and track their commission. The owner watches leads, OTP-proved visits, tokens, the brokerage ledger, agent performance and rent renewals at 11 months, live.

Architecture

  • One person, one lead. Every enquiry is checked by phone (the last ten digits, so +91 55440 55123, 5544055123 and 055440 55123 are the same person). A second enquiry is held as a duplicate until it is merged; the merged lead keeps every enquiry with its source and listing, and a lead lost earlier reopens as a returning client. Only then does round robin pick the agent, so two agents never chase the same buyer.
  • Visits are proved, not claimed. Booking a visit makes a 4-digit OTP that only the buyer gets. The agent's check-in needs that OTP and the phone's location; the distance to the listing (or the project) is worked out and stored with the visit, and the owner, the office and agent performance all read the same proof. GPS is simulated in this demo.
  • Money is worked out from the deal. The token opens a deal at the agreed price with its schedule: resale (token, booking to 10%, agreement 10%, the rest at registration), rental (token, deposit balance and first rent) or the builder's construction-linked plan. Brokerage is 1% from each side on resale, a month's rent from each side on a rental and the builder's commission on a project unit; a channel partner's share is set aside. Due is always the share minus what was received, never typed in.
  • Project prices from one sheet. A unit's price is its area × (base rate + floor rise above the 2nd floor + corner PLC). Change the sheet and all 96 units re-price; the tower grid shows available, on hold (with who and until when), booked and sold.
  • Static pages only. Built with Next.js on FrameMind's shared core as a static export, served from framemindaistudio.com/apps/deal. No server, no sign-up: the demo keeps sample data in the visitor's browser and updates other tabs live; a paying agency gets a real backend behind the same data interface, its portal accounts and a WhatsApp number.
  • Shared core. Dummy logins and route guards for five roles, feature switches with tiers, demo UPI payments, printable brochures, agreement drafts, brokerage invoices and receipts, simulated WhatsApp to leads, and English / हिन्दी / ಕನ್ನಡ.

Workflow

  1. 1. Listing added with photos, video and floor plans
  2. 2. Leads come in from the website, ads and portals; duplicates merged
  3. 3. Lead auto-assigned; call or WhatsApp in one tap
  4. 4. Site visit booked; OTP and GPS prove it happened
  5. 5. Feedback, negotiation, token and booking
  6. 6. Agreement documents and brokerage collected
  7. 7. After sale: rent renewals and repeat business
