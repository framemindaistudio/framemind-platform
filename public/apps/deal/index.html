How it works

  1. 1Listing added with photos, video and floor plans
  2. 2Leads come in from the website, ads and portals; duplicates merged
  3. 3Lead auto-assigned; call or WhatsApp in one tap
  4. 4Site visit booked; OTP and GPS prove it happened
  5. 5Feedback, negotiation, token and booking
  6. 6Agreement documents and brokerage collected
  7. 7After sale: rent renewals and repeat business

Buyer / tenant
Ananya Rao (home buyer)

Search homes to buy or rent with filters and a map, photos, video tours, floor plans and an EMI calculator; enquire, book a site visit and get the visit OTP; give feedback, make an offer, pay the token and follow the payment schedule and rent renewal.

Property owner
Venkatesh Murthy (owner)

List a flat, villa or plot with photos, video and a floor plan; see enquiries, every site visit with its OTP and GPS proof, the buyers' feedback and offers to accept or counter; follow the token, payments and rent renewals.

Agent
Kavya Reddy (agent)

Today's follow-ups and visits, leads assigned by round robin with a score, one-tap call and WhatsApp, the pipeline from new to booked, visit slots with reminders, OTP and GPS proof at the site, feedback, offers, token and booking, listings with portal publishing, and rent renewals.

Channel partner
Imran Sheikh (Keystone Realtors)

Register a client for a project unit or a listing (protected for 60 days), see live project availability, follow each client's visits and booking, and track commission earned, due and paid.

Owner / admin
Prakash Hegde (owner)

The whole agency live: one lead list from every source, duplicates to merge, auto-assignment, portal and ad import, the project's tower and unit grid, listings on portals, the brokerage ledger, agent performance, channel partner commissions and rent renewals.

Portal publishing, portal and ad lead import, GPS at the site and WhatsApp to leads are simulated and labelled.
