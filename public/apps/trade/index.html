The B2B Ordering Network for Distributors & Retailers.

You're looking at Sahyadri Distributors, a sample business in Bengaluru. A staples distributor on the TradeOS network. Its shops order from it — and from their other distributors — in one free app.

Plan builder How we built this app

How it works

  1. 1Distributor adds its shops by mobile number; a shop already on TradeOS is linked, never duplicated
  2. 2The shop sees the products of every distributor that added it, in one list
  3. 3One cart; on checkout it splits into one order per distributor
  4. 4Each distributor accepts, unticks what it can't send, or declines with a reason
  5. 5Dispatch and delivery; the shop follows each step live
  6. 6Reorder from history in one tap; salespeople book orders on shop visits
