01

Brief

A kirana in Jayanagar buys from four or five distributors: staples from one, soaps from another, biscuits and cold drinks from a third. Each distributor that went digital gave the shop its own app, so the shopkeeper juggles several apps, several logins and several WhatsApp threads — or keeps calling the salesman.

TradeOS turns that into one network. The shop installs one free app and sees the products of every distributor that added it, in one list, each in that distributor's own colours. It orders everything in one cart; on checkout the cart splits into one order per distributor. Each distributor accepts, unticks what it can't send or declines with a reason, dispatches, and the shop follows every step live. Salespeople book orders on their beat, and the delivery app closes each drop with the shop's OTP or a photo.

The founder's rule shaped the whole build: the core has no prices, invoices or stock — products are simply available or not available. Price lists, GST invoices, stock, credit, routes, Tally, WhatsApp and AI are optional switches, off in the default plan and fully working in this demo when you switch them on.

Architecture

  • One network, not one app per distributor. A shop is one record keyed by its mobile number. A distributor never creates a second copy: adding a mobile that is already on TradeOS creates a link between that distributor and the existing shop. The network team's screen checks the rule (no two shops share a mobile).
  • Cart → orders. A checkout carries one cart id; the order service writes one order per distributor with that id, so each distributor sees only its part while the shop sees the whole cart together.
  • Price-free core, optional layers. Products carry an available / not available switch. Rates, schemes, stock per warehouse, credit limits and receipts sit in the data but every screen shows them only when their feature is switched on in the plan builder.
  • Static pages only. Built with Next.js on FrameMind's shared core as a static export, served from framemindaistudio.com/apps/trade. No server, no sign-up: the demo keeps sample data in the visitor's browser and updates other tabs live; a paying client gets a real backend behind the same data interface.
  • Shared core. Dummy logins and route guards for five roles, feature switches with tiers, the plan builder, demo UPI payments, the simulated WhatsApp outbox, printable GST invoices and English / हिन्दी / ಕನ್ನಡ.
06

Workflow

  1. 1. Distributor adds its shops by mobile number; a shop already on TradeOS is linked, never duplicated
  2. 2. The shop sees the products of every distributor that added it, in one list
  3. 3. One cart; on checkout it splits into one order per distributor
  4. 4. Each distributor accepts, unticks what it can't send, or declines with a reason
  5. 5. Dispatch and delivery; the shop follows each step live
  6. 6. Reorder from history in one tap; salespeople book orders on shop visits
