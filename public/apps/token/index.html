No more waiting rooms full of guesswork.

You're looking at Tulasi Family Clinic, a sample business in Bengaluru. A family clinic in HSR Layout with a second branch in Koramangala: five doctors in morning and evening sessions, about 80 patients a day, its own pharmacy and a partner lab.

How it works

  1. 1Patient books a token from home, or reception adds a walk-in
  2. 2Live queue on the phone: people ahead and wait time
  3. 3Doctor calls the next patient; notes, vitals and prescription
  4. 4Prescription on WhatsApp; follow-up date set
  5. 5Bill and payment; pharmacy dispenses
  6. 6Follow-up reminder; owner's day report

Try every role

Each login opens only that role's screens, just like the real product.

Patient
Ananya Rao (patient)

Book a token from home within the day's limit (or a video consult), watch the live queue with people ahead and the wait, get the prescription on WhatsApp, pay the bill by UPI, see past visits and reports, book the follow-up and link an ABHA health ID.

Reception
Divya Shetty (front desk)

Today's tokens per doctor: mark arrived, on hold or no-show, add walk-ins and phone bookings to the same queue, record vitals and reports, collect bills, send lab samples to the partner lab and remind follow-ups.

Doctor
Dr. Meera Hegde (physician)

Call the next patient in one tap, skip, hold and recall; the patient's history, vitals and reports; visit notes, an e-prescription from templates, lab orders, the follow-up date and video consults.

Pharmacy
Rizwan Pasha (pharmacist)

Prescriptions arrive from the doctor's room: dispense from stock, bill at MRP and take payment; low stock, reorder levels, expiry dates and stock received.

Owner
Dr. Raghavendra Kamath (owner)

The day report live: patients by doctor, no-shows, waiting times and collections by consultation, lab and pharmacy; doctors, sessions and daily token limits; both branches side by side.
