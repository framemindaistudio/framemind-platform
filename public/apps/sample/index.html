From sample to report, tracked.

You're looking at Nandadeepa Diagnostics, a sample business in Bengaluru. A pathology lab in Basavanagudi with collection centres in JP Nagar and Banashankari: four collection partners on two-wheelers, five analysers, a pathologist who signs every report, doctors who refer patients, and nursing homes and companies billed on credit.

How it works

  1. 1Test or package booked, home or lab visit
  2. 2Collection partner assigned; patient tracks arrival
  3. 3Sample barcoded and received at the lab
  4. 4Results entered with normal ranges; abnormal values flagged
  5. 5Pathologist checks and signs
  6. 6Report PDF on the phone and WhatsApp; trends over time

Try every role

Each login opens only that role's screens, just like the real product.

Patient
Rekha Murthy (patient)

Book a test or package with the price upfront, at home in a time slot or at a centre; pay by UPI; watch the collection partner's ETA; share the sample code at the door; get the report on the phone with every value's range, high and low marked, a PDF and trends over past reports.

Collection partner
Santosh Kumar (partner)

Today's home collections in slot order: start the trip (the patient sees the ETA), check the sample code, draw the tubes the tests need, print barcode labels, collect cash or UPI, and hand the bag and cash over at the lab.

Lab technician
Imran Sheikh (technician)

Receive tubes by scanning their barcodes, draw lab-visit patients, run the worklist by department with turnaround-time alerts, pull values from the analysers (simulated) or type them, with normal ranges and high / low flags as you enter.

Pathologist
Dr. Sharada Rao (pathologist)

Reports waiting to be signed, critical values first; every value against its range and the patient's past results; add a remark, send a test back for a rerun, or sign and release the report to the patient and the doctor.

Referring doctor
Dr. Prakash Hegde (doctor)

Refer a patient for tests from the clinic, follow each sample to the report, open signed reports with trends the moment they're released, and see referral commissions for the month and past payouts.

Owner
Raghavendra Bhat (owner)

The lab live: bookings, samples at every stage, partners on the road, turnaround breaches, today's collection and dues; daily and monthly reports; B2B clients on credit with statements; referring doctors' commissions; the three centres side by side; the catalogue and prices.

The analyser interface, WhatsApp / SMS and UPI are simulated and labelled. Try it across tabs: book a package with home collection as the patient, start the trip as the collection partner, receive the tubes at the lab, sign as the pathologist, and watch the report land on the patient's phone and the doctor's portal while the owner's dashboard moves.
