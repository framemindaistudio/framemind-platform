01

Brief

A salon sells time in a chair, a room or on a machine, and it lives on clients who come back. Most salons and spas in Bengaluru run on a diary or a WhatsApp group, a billing app that knows nothing about the booking, and the stylist's memory. So two clients are booked on the one hair-spa steamer, a couples massage is promised when only one spa room is free, walk-ins are told “ten minutes” for forty, the colour that suited a client last month is a guess, the membership balance lives in a register, and nobody reminds the client that her roots are due.

GlowOS runs the salon around the booking that holds people, rooms and machines together. The client books online by service and stylist (or anyone free) at a time when the person, a station of the right kind and any machine are all free; a couples massage needs two therapists and two spa rooms. The calendar shows every stylist, chair, room and machine with each booking as long as its service. Reminders go out before the visit (automatically on WhatsApp with the add-on) and no-shows are tracked; repeat no-shows are flagged. Walk-ins join a queue with a real wait worked out from the bookings.

At the chair the stylist opens the client card: history, notes, allergies and every colour formula; finishes the service with notes, the formula and before and after photos. The desk bills services and products together; packages cover their sessions, the membership balance and gift cards pay first and the rest goes by UPI. The bill sends a review request and sets a rebooking reminder for when the service is due again. Stock moves with every sale, and the owner sees sales, commissions against targets, balances still to be used and both branches live.

Architecture

  • One engine for every booking. Each service has a length, a kind of station (hair chair, nail station, facial room, spa room) and sometimes a machine (hair-spa steamer, hydra-facial machine). A time is open only when a person with the skill, a free station of that kind and the machine are all free for the whole length; a couples service asks for two of each. Online bookings, desk bookings, walk-ins and the queue's waits all use the same check, so nothing can be double-booked.
  • The queue is worked out, not kept. Each walk-in's wait is the earliest 5-minute step at which someone capable, a station and any machine are free, after today's bookings and after the people ahead. Calling a walk-in in makes a booking on the spot.
  • Balances are sums, never stored. A membership's balance is its value minus every bill it paid; a package's sessions are its count minus every bill line it covered; a gift card's balance likewise. So the client app, the desk and the owner always agree. Commission is each person's rate on services at list price (also when a package paid) plus 5% on products they sold.
  • The bill closes the loop. Saving a bill marks the services billed, takes products out of stock, issues memberships, packages and gift cards sold, sends the review request and sets a rebooking reminder per service at its own interval (roots 35 days, a haircut 6 weeks).
  • Static pages only. Built with Next.js on FrameMind's shared core as a static export, served from framemindaistudio.com/apps/glow. No server and no sign-up: the demo keeps sample data in the visitor's browser, updates other tabs live and starts a fresh day each morning. A paying salon gets a real backend behind the same data interface, a UPI gateway and WhatsApp.
  • Shared core. Dummy logins and route guards for four roles, feature switches with tiers, demo UPI payments, printable bills, day reports and commission sheets, simulated SMS / WhatsApp, and English / हिन्दी / ಕನ್ನಡ.

Workflow

  2. 2. Reminder before the visit
  3. 3. Service done; notes, formula and photos saved
  4. 4. Bill with services and products; membership balance used
  5. 5. Review request and rebooking reminder
