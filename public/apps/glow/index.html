How it works

  1. 1Client books a service with a stylist, or walks in
  2. 2Reminder before the visit
  3. 3Service done; notes, formula and photos saved
  4. 4Bill with services and products; membership balance used
  5. 5Review request and rebooking reminder

Reception

The day on one calendar of stylists, rooms and machines: check clients in, run the walk-in queue with waits, send reminders and mark no-shows, bill services and products with membership balance, packages and gift cards, sell memberships, and send review and rebooking reminders.

Stylist / therapist
Ritu Sharma (senior stylist)

My day with durations; open the client card with past visits, notes, allergies and colour formulas; start and finish the service, save notes, the formula and before and after photos; see my commission against the month's target and my reviews.

Owner
Nandini Rao (owner)

The salon live: sales today and this month, services against products, chairs and rooms in use, no-shows, membership balances and gift cards still to be used, low stock, each stylist's commission against target, reviews, and both branches side by side.
