How it works

  1. 1Enquiry or walk-in gets a free trial pass and follow-up
  2. 2Joins a plan and pays; membership card on the phone
  3. 3Checks in with a QR at the desk or kiosk
  4. 4Books classes or PT sessions; follows the workout and diet plan
  5. 5Trainer logs progress: weight, measurements, photos
  6. 6Renewal reminders before expiry; freeze, upgrade or transfer
