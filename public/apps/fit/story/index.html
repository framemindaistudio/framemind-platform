01

Brief

A neighbourhood gym in Bengaluru runs on a register at the desk, a WhatsApp group for class timings and a spreadsheet of who has paid. Walk-ins ask about a trial and are never called back, members slip past the desk after their plan has ended, renewals are chased by phone after they lapse, part payments live in someone's memory and trainers keep progress notes on their own phones. The owner only finds out the month was weak when the rent is due.

FitOS runs the whole member journey in one app. An enquiry from the website or a walk-in gets a free trial pass and dated follow-ups. The person joins a plan with its duration, price and joining fee — paying in full or in part — and gets a membership card with a QR on the phone. They check in by showing the QR at the desk or typing their number on the kiosk; an expired or frozen card is turned away. They book classes with capacity and a waitlist that moves up by itself, buy PT packages that count down session by session, and follow the workout and diet plan their trainer built from the exercise library, logging every set. The trainer logs weight, measurements and progress photos, and the owner sees it all live.

Before a plan ends the desk sends renewal reminders (7, 3 and 1 day out) and the member renews in one tap; memberships can be frozen, upgraded with credit for unused days, or transferred. A supplement counter, trainer commissions, staff attendance, challenges with leaderboards and a second branch where the same card works complete the picture. Door access / biometric needs hardware, so it is simulated and labelled in this demo, and WhatsApp messages land in the demo outbox instead of being sent.

05

Architecture

  • One member record, many states. A member holds a plan, a start and an end date, dues, an optional freeze and an optional PT balance. Active, due soon (7 days), expired, frozen and transferred are worked out from those dates every time, never stored, so the desk, the door and the reports can't disagree.
  • Money rules in one place. Prices include 18% GST and receipts show the split. Part payments leave dues on the card; renewals start from the old end date if it hasn't passed; an upgrade credits the unused days of the current plan; a freeze pushes the end date out by the frozen days; a transfer moves the remaining validity to a new person for a fee. Trainer commission = PT package sales × PT rate + counter sales (ex-GST) × shop rate.
  • Check-in from three doors. The member's QR on the desk scanner, the mobile number on the kiosk, or a fingerprint at the door controller (simulated) all call the same check: card valid, not frozen, not already in today. Trial passes check in the same way until they expire. Every branch accepts every card.
  • Static pages only. Built with Next.js on FrameMind's shared core as a static export, served from framemindaistudio.com/apps/fit. No server, no sign-up: the demo keeps sample data in the visitor's browser and updates other tabs live; a paying gym gets a real backend behind the same data interface.
  • Shared core. Dummy logins and route guards for four roles, feature switches with tiers, demo UPI payments, printable receipts and reports, and English / हिन्दी / ಕನ್ನಡ. The volt-orange accent is too light for small white text, so accent buttons carry navy text.
06

Workflow

  1. 1. Enquiry or walk-in gets a free trial pass and follow-up
  2. 2. Joins a plan and pays; membership card on the phone
  3. 3. Checks in with a QR at the desk or kiosk
  4. 4. Books classes or PT sessions; follows the workout and diet plan
  5. 5. Trainer logs progress: weight, measurements, photos
  6. 6. Renewal reminders before expiry; freeze, upgrade or transfer
