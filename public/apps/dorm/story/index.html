01

Brief

A PG or hostel in Bengaluru runs on a notebook of beds, a WhatsApp group for notices and a spreadsheet of who has paid. Vacancies are posted on five sites and the calls go to the owner's phone; tokens are taken in cash with no record of which bed was promised; ID copies sit in a drawer and the police verification form is never filed. Every month someone walks up to each room's meter, works out the electricity share on a calculator and chases rent on the phone. Complaints are shouted across the corridor, the kitchen cooks for forty when twenty-eight eat, parents call the warden at night to ask if their daughter is in, and the deposit refund at move-out ends in an argument.

DormOS runs the whole stay in one app. The public vacancy page shows live beds by sharing and rent, and a visitor books a visit from it. The warden marks the visit and books a bed with a token deposit paid by UPI. The new resident uploads KYC and fills the police verification form on the phone; the warden verifies them and generates the rent agreement, which the resident e-signs with Aadhaar (simulated in this demo). On the day, the warden ticks the move-in checklist, adds room photos and the meter reading and collects the balance deposit. Each month the warden enters room meter readings; DormOS splits the units between the room's residents and puts each share on the rent invoice, which the resident pays by UPI or UPI AutoPay (simulated) and gets a receipt. Reminders and the late fee follow the property's rule.

During the stay residents raise complaints with photos that go to housekeeping and back with a status, skip meals so the kitchen cooks the right count, ask for leave and gate passes, and read notices. Hostels add night roll call, a visitor log and a parent app where parents confirm leave and pay fees. At the end the resident gives notice, the warden does the move-out inspection, and the deposit ledger works out the refund. The owner watches both properties live: occupancy, collection, dues ageing, expenses, profit and staff.

Names considered

FrameMind DormOS says rooms and beds in four letters and fits PGs, hostels, co-living and student housing. It follows the family rule (FrameMind <Word>OS, “OS” in the accent).

  • DormOS — chosen — PGs, hostels and co-living alike
  • StayOS — taken — already used by a hospitality product
  • NestOS — considered — soft and homely, but reads as a smart-home brand
  • RoomOS — rejected — Cisco's name for its video-device operating system

Architecture

  • Property → floor → room → bed. Every bed belongs to a room on a floor of a property. A bed's state (vacant, booked, occupied, on notice) is worked out from the resident who holds it, never stored, so the bed map, the vacancy page and the owner's occupancy can't disagree. A new property is added with its floors, rooms and sharing, and its beds appear everywhere at once.
  • One resident record from enquiry to refund. Booking creates the resident with the bed held and the token on the deposit ledger; KYC, the police form, the agreement and its e-sign, the move-in checklist with photos, the notice and the move-out settlement are all steps on the same record.
  • Money rules in one place. Rent includes food and carries no GST (residential rent is exempt). A meter reading turns the room's units × the property's rate into equal shares for the residents living there; each share waits until that resident's next invoice. A first month is pro-rated from the move-in day. The late fee is per day after the due date, capped. The deposit held = token + deposit − deductions − refunds, and the refund at move-out = deposit held − unpaid invoices − inspection deductions.
  • Static pages only. Built with Next.js on FrameMind's shared core as a static export, served from framemindaistudio.com/apps/dorm. No server, no sign-up: the demo keeps sample data in the visitor's browser and updates other tabs live; a paying PG gets a real backend behind the same data interface, a real UPI gateway and AutoPay mandates, and an Aadhaar e-sign provider.
  • Shared core. Dummy logins and route guards for five roles, feature switches with tiers, demo UPI payments, printable invoices, agreements, police forms and reports, and English / हिन्दी / ಕನ್ನಡ. The key-gold accent is too light for small white text, so accent buttons carry navy text.

Workflow

  1. 1. Enquiry from the public vacancy page; visit booked
  2. 2. Bed booked with a token deposit
  3. 3. Digital KYC, police verification form and rent agreement
  4. 4. Move-in checklist with room photos
  5. 5. Rent invoice each month with the electricity share; pay by UPI
  6. 6. Complaints, meals, leave and gate passes during the stay
  7. 7. Notice period, move-out inspection and deposit refund
