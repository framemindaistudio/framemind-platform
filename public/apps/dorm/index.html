Every bed, every rupee, every request.

You're looking at Nest Stay, a sample business in Bengaluru. A men's PG for working professionals in HSR Layout (24 beds) and a girls' hostel for students in Jayanagar (23 beds), both with food. One warden app runs both; parents of hostel students follow attendance and payments.

How it works

  1. 1Enquiry from the public vacancy page; visit booked
  2. 2Bed booked with a token deposit
  3. 3Digital KYC, police verification form and rent agreement
  4. 4Move-in checklist with room photos
  5. 5Rent invoice each month with the electricity share; pay by UPI
  6. 6Complaints, meals, leave and gate passes during the stay
  7. 7Notice period, move-out inspection and deposit refund
