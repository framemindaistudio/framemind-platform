How it works

  1. 1The shop opens one free appIt sees the products of every distributor it buys from — FMCG, electrical, building materials — in one list
  2. 2Orders everything in one goNo prices to compare, just what's available and how much
  3. 3Each distributor gets its own orderThe app splits the cart and sends every distributor only its part
  4. 4Distributors accept, dispatch, deliverThe shop follows each order live; salespeople can book orders too
